# Contributing

Every contribution must identify the research question it serves, distinguish
observation from hypothesis and conclusion, preserve determinism, include tests
where possible, expose its evidence sources and avoid hidden assumptions.

Work that does not help explain, reproduce or predict wallet discovery is
outside this project's scope.
